Seven recent publications cover peptide mapping, modified-RNA annotation, reproducible metabolomics, ion-mobility imaging and multi-omics tools.

Prioritize efficient mapping of ambiguous sequences and executable, auditable MS workflows. Coverage remains incomplete because some ACS, MCP and Nature sources were inaccessible; no paper software was independently run.

Wild-AC: A fast, index-free multi-pattern string matching algorithm with wildcard support for proteomics

Chris Bielow

BMC Bioinformatics · 2026

First online:
2026-10-09
DOI:
10.1186/s12859-026-06686-8
Publication status:
Peer-reviewed accepted manuscript / Article in Press, before final editing.

Wild-AC extends Aho–Corasick with branching and pruning to map peptide sets onto protein sequences with ambiguous residues, without a protein-database index. Benchmarks favor large query sets, while FM-index can win for small sets; consecutive wildcards can cause exponential branching, requiring limits and pruning. This is sequence mapping, not raw-spectrum identification or FDR estimation.

Reading scope: Publisher accepted manuscript, methods and benchmarks read; code not rerun. Timings exclude FM-index construction.

RNabel─A Standalone Software Tool for Annotating Tandem Mass Spectra of Modified Ribonucleic Acids

Ge Song; Yi-Jie Nathan Du; Rui-Xiang Sun; Meng-Qiu Dong

Analytical Chemistry · 2026

First online:
2026-09-28
DOI:
10.1021/acs.analchem.6c02176
Publication status:
Journal version following preprint 10.64898/2026.06.22.733900, posted June 24, 2026.

RNabel is standalone Mac/Windows software for annotating modified RNA-oligonucleotide tandem spectra. It supports nine backbone-ion types, 196 editable modifications, custom residues/losses and batch imports, with annotation-quality statistics. The accessible text supports a flexible annotation workflow; it does not establish de novo sequencing or globally validated false-discovery control.

Reading scope: Official abstract and public supplementary description; full text and software not evaluated.

Metabonaut: an open educational resource for learning reproducible, script-based untargeted LC-MS/MS metabolomics

Philippine Louail; Marilyn De Graeve; Alexandru Mahmoud; Daniel Marques de Sá e Silva; Kozo Nishida; Vilhelm Suksi; Anna Tagliaferri; Gabriele Tomè; Vinicius Verri Hernandes; Johannes Rainer

Metabolomics · 2026

First online:
2026-10-03
DOI:
10.1007/s11306-026-02538-x
Publication status:
Peer-reviewed methods / educational review.

Metabonaut teaches reproducible untargeted LC-MS/MS analysis through nine executable R/Bioconductor vignettes covering preprocessing, QC, annotation, alignment and standardized export, with Python/SIRIUS interoperability. This is versioned, containerized training rather than a new algorithm. Its imperfect teaching dataset illustrates how mismatched QC samples can worsen correction; workflows were not independently rerun.

Reading scope: Publisher main text, workflow design and limitations read; no workflow rerun.

From Pixels to Chemical Knowledge: Interpreting Ion Mobility-Enabled Multidimensional Mass Spectrometry Imaging

Kittisak Taoma; Pattipong Wisanpitayakorn; Kwanjeera Wanichthanarak; David R. Gang; Sakda Khoomrung

ACS Measurement Science Au · 2026

First online:
2026-10-06
DOI:
10.1021/acsmeasuresciau.6c00282
Publication status:
Peer-reviewed methods / educational review.

This methods review compares per-pixel and averaged-spectrum preprocessing for four-dimensional ion-mobility MSI, including spatially informed filtering and sensitivity/runtime tradeoffs. It maps mobility-preserving data-access gaps and CCS-supported annotation, separating existing tools from proposed AI extensions. Its practical value is workflow design and interoperability guidance; it introduces no newly validated algorithm.

Reading scope: Selected publisher-indexed main text on data access, preprocessing and CCS annotation; cited tools not retested.

TurbOmics: a web-based platform for the analysis of metabolomics data using a multi-omics integrative approach

Rafael Barrero-Rodríguez; Jose Manuel Rodríguez; Thomas Naake; Wolfgang Huber; María Juárez-Fernández; Almudena R. Ramiro; Jesús Vázquez; Annalaura Mastrangelo; Alessia Ferrarini

Genome Biology · 2026

First online:
2026-10-10
DOI:
10.1186/s13059-026-04272-y
Publication status:
Peer-reviewed accepted manuscript / Article in Press, before the final Version of Record.

TurbOmics integrates CMM/TurboPutative annotations, MOFA and PathIntegrate for combined metabolomic, proteomic and transcriptomic analysis. Reanalysis examples demonstrate the workflow, but integration requires paired samples, putative annotations are not confirmed identities, and additional candidates do not establish superiority under matched error control.

Reading scope: Official article page and selected accepted-manuscript results, methods and limitations; supplements and software not audited.

ThermoTargetMiner as a proteome integral solubility alteration target database for prospective drugs against lung cancer

Hezheng Lyu; Hassan Gharibi; Bohdana Sokolova; Mücahit Varli; Anna Voiland; Brady Nilsson; Zhaowei Meng; Massimiliano Gaetani; Amir Ata Saei; Roman A. Zubarev

Communications Chemistry · 2026

First online:
2026-10-02
DOI:
10.1038/s42004-026-02207-z
Publication status:
Peer-reviewed journal article.

Across 67 compounds and four PISA contexts, ThermoTargetMiner combines normalized TMT LC-MS/MS measurements with drug-versus-other-drugs OPLS-DA and control-tuned outlier filtering. Recurrence supports prioritization, but the chosen k=2 criterion is operational: randomized-overlap analysis favored k≥3 for statistical reliability. Outlier significance and recurrence do not establish direct binding or calibrated target-level FDR.

Reading scope: Publisher main text and statistical methods read; supplementary data and application not independently tested.

PSA-GUI: A Windows-Based Graphical User Interface for the Projected Superposition Approximation Collision Cross-Section Calculation

Farhad Zahedi; Christian Bleiholder

Journal of Mass Spectrometry · 2026

First online:
2026-09-28
DOI:
10.1002/jms.70116
Publication status:
Journal version following ChemRxiv preprint 10.26434/chemrxiv.15002715/v1, posted May 5, 2026.

PSA-GUI packages established projected superposition approximation CCS calculations in a Windows/WSL workflow. It combines parameter setup, PDB/XYZ import, conformer extraction, resource-aware batch runs and live monitoring, making model-to-ion-mobility comparisons easier to organize. The accessible abstract does not demonstrate improved CCS accuracy or numerical speedups.

Reading scope: Journal abstract, PubMed and publisher-deposited metadata; full text not read.
