Latest2026-10-24 · Beijing
English中文

Today's papers connect complementary evidence to mass spectrometry: image-guided sampling through OpenDVP, long-read-informed protein isoform analysis through LRP2, and cross-link-guided structural modeling. Their reusable components address different analytical stages, with important limits on spatial resolution, identification evidence and structural validation.

The check also covered accessible publisher listings and indexed records for small molecules, lipidomics, imaging, ion mobility, signal processing and modified peptides. Some ACS, MCP, PROTEOMICS and preprint pages were inaccessible or had incomplete metadata; coverage is not exhaustive.

OpenDVP: an experimental and computational framework for community-empowered deep visual proteomics

Jose Nimo; Sonja Fritzsche; Daniela S. Valdes; Minh Trinh; Tancredi Pentimalli; Melissa Klingeberg; Simon Schallenberg; Frederick Klauschen; Florian Herse; Stefan Florian; Nikolaus Rajewsky; Fabian Coscia

Nature Methods · 2026

First online:
2026-10-22
DOI:
10.1038/s41592-026-03262-z
Publication status:
Version of record

OpenDVP connects image processing, cell phenotyping and neighborhood analysis, laser microdissection, and MS proteomics through modular AnnData/SpatialData interfaces. The authors demonstrate three archival tissue applications and segment 6.34 million cells across 11 whole-slide images in under two hours; in a matched triple-negative breast cancer benchmark, neighborhood sampling accelerates tissue collection about 100-fold and increases protein identifications by an average of 26% relative to pooled individual-cell contours. The interfaces and sampling design are reusable, but neighborhoods sacrifice cellular resolution, and results depend on staining, segmentation and collection quality rather than establishing universal gains.

LRP2: A proteogenomics pipeline for long-read informed protein isoform analysis and discovery

Megan D Schertzer; Julia T Lewandowski; Emily F Watts; Will Rosenow; Madison M Mehlferber; Erin D Jeffery; Scott I Adamson; Jocelyne Bruand; Elizabeth Tseng; Yaseswini Neelamraju; Francine E Garrett-Bakelman; Egor Dolzhenko; David A Knowles; Gloria Sheynkman

Bioinformatics · 2026

First online:
2026-10-23
DOI:
10.1093/bioinformatics/btag790
Publication status:
Accepted manuscript

LRP2 integrates long-read transcript discovery, artifact filtering, open-reading-frame prediction, protein isoform classification, differential analysis, and FragPipe DDA/DIA evidence in a Nextflow workflow. Deterministic splice-junction and coordinate-based identifiers support cross-dataset comparisons, while the abstract reports scaling to hundreds of samples and supporting novel peptide discovery and isoform inference. It could reduce workflow assembly costs, but the abstract provides no quantitative benchmark establishing superior identification accuracy.

Reading scope: Publisher abstract and metadata only; no reproduction

Cross-linking mass spectrometry guided structure prediction of disordered Encephalitozoon hellem proteins

Elizabeth Weyer; Yongliang Wang; Tadakimi Tomita; Carlos Madrid-Aliste; Prithviraj Nandigrami; Andras Fiser; Simone Sidoli; Jennifer T. Aguilan; Bing Han; Louis M. Weiss

mBio · 2026

First online:
2026-10-20
DOI:
10.1128/mbio.02261-26
Publication status:
Journal article

This study combines AlphaLink monomer modeling with MODELLER refinement and docking, incorporating XL-MS distance restraints throughout a reusable workflow for low-homology, flexible protein complexes and supplying accompanying scripts. Independently generated cross-links and restraint-removal tests support the Swp1b–EnP1 model, but validation centers on one complex: agreement with restraints does not establish atomic accuracy or broad generalization.

Reading scope: Publisher-indexed abstract and main-text excerpts; live article unavailable; no reproduction
