Accounting for longitudinal peak quality metrics with MSstats+ enhances differential analysis in proteomic experiments with data-independent acquisition

Devon Kohler; Eralp Dogu; Mrittika Bhattacharya; Ozge Karayel; Manuel Magana; Anthony Wu; Veronica G. Anania; Olga Vitek

Nature Communications · 2026

First online:
2026-10-17
DOI:
10.1038/s41467-026-78591-w
Publication status:
Accepted article; early version

MSstats+ combines DIA intensities with peak-quality metrics and their run-order profiles to downweight unreliable measurements, including imputed values, during differential analysis. This journal version expands the abstract’s validation from four experiments to five, including a second clinical study, and explicitly includes DIA-NN alongside Spectronaut; quantitative gains and detailed validation conditions were not verified from full text.

MassBank Human: The annotated human metabolome

Fumio Matsuda; Ryosuke Hayasaka; Taihei Torigoe; Masatomo Takahashi; Yushi Takahashi; Akiyasu C. Yoshizawa; Hiroshi Tsugawa; Shujiro Okuda; Steffen Neumann; Emma L. Schymanski; Yoshihiro Izumi; Akiyoshi Hirayama

bioRxiv · 2026

First online:
2026-10-16
DOI:
10.64898/2026.10.10.758200
Publication status:
Preprint, version 1; not peer reviewed

MassBank Human condenses 36.5 million spectra from 76 public human datasets into 203,886 curated spectra and combines three annotation tools through Zipf-Bayesian rank aggregation with reported FDR control. The preprint reports 14,198 spectra with planar-structure annotations and an increase in colorectal-cancer-cell annotation coverage from 10.6% to 21.2%. Reusable spectra and uncertainty-aware aggregation are promising, but spectrum counts are not unique compounds or confirmed identities, and the FDR calibration and library–test independence remain unchecked here.

Reading scope: bioRxiv abstract, metadata and repository README; manuscript not read

Beyond Co-Localization: DeepCoEx Learns Exclusion-Aware Spatial Representations in Mass Spectrometry Imaging via Dual-Contrastive Learning

Jingjing Xu; Xin Diao; Siyang Liu; Thomas Ka-Yam Lam; Yusong Zhang; Shuang Gao; Lingli Deng; Weiji Chen; Lei Guo; Zongwei Cai; Jiyang Dong

Analytical Chemistry · 2026

First online:
2026-10-16
DOI:
10.1021/acs.analchem.6c02338
Publication status:
Peer-reviewed journal version, online ahead of issue

DeepCoEx uses dual-contrastive learning to retain spatial consistency while distinguishing complementary ion distributions, reducing reliance on labeled negative pairs. The abstract reports more consistent retrieval than conventional similarity and dimensionality-reduction baselines, illustrated in rat brain and barley seeds. Modeling spatial exclusion alongside co-localization is useful, but these associations do not establish biochemical antagonism; numerical performance and robustness were not verified here.

Reading scope: Publisher-deposited abstract and metadata; full text not read

Pretrained spectrum representations for diverse tandem mass spectrometry proteomics tasks

Justin Sanders; Melih Yilmaz; Jacob H. Russell; Wout Bittremieux; William E. Fondrie; Nicholas M. Riley; Sewoong Oh; William S. Noble

First online:
2026-10-07

10.1016/j.mcpro.2026.101673
Publication status:
Journal pre-proof

The Casanovo-based framework reuses a de novo sequencing encoder for spectrum-quality, chimericity, phosphorylation and glycosylation prediction, then improves task performance through fine-tuning. These are spectrum-level prediction tasks rather than demonstrated improvements in identification or PTM-site localization; the abstract does not establish transfer across arbitrary instruments or acquisition settings.

Empirical-Bayes and Bayesian Hierarchical Modelling for Missingness and Differential Expression in Proteomics

Mengchun Li; Venkatesh Mallikarjun; Andrew Frey; Emmanuel O. Ogundimu; Matthias Trost

Molecular & Cellular Proteomics · 2026

First online:
2026-10-07
DOI:
10.1016/j.mcpro.2026.101677
Publication status:
Journal pre-proof

missBayes models observed intensities and intensity-dependent missingness jointly, using empirical Bayes estimation and posterior sampling rather than filling in fixed values before differential analysis. The publisher reports comparison against ten workflows; inference remains conditional on the missingness model, and the accessible text does not establish universally better performance or computational cost.

Unlabelled Spatial Metabolic Covariance Analysis (US-MCA) Reveals Tumor-Associated Reorganization of Interorgan Choline Metabolic Response Patterns in Lung Cancer

Xiaoyu Fan; Xiangyi Wang; Rong Yan; Yiran Zhao; Hongtao Jin; Jiuming He

Analytical Chemistry · 2026

First online:
2026-10-16
DOI:
10.1021/acs.analchem.6c03732
Publication status:
Peer-reviewed journal version, online ahead of issue

US-MCA combines whole-body AFADESI-MSI with perturbation-based, semi-quantitative covariance analysis to compare interorgan metabolic responses. D9-choline tracing supported concordant patterns for reliably detected choline-derived signals, with limited coverage of downstream one-carbon metabolites. The transferable element is spatial response coordination; the study does not establish biochemical flux, instantaneous rates or direct interorgan transport.

Parameter-Based Prioritization of Time-Resolved LC–HRMS Ion Tracks for Untargeted Reaction Monitoring

Igor Ilyasov; Arcady Braun; Vera Olicheva; Valery Goman; Victoria Grikh; Anastasiya Zhevlakova; Irina Selivanova; Vladimir Beloborodov

International Journal of Molecular Sciences · 2026

First online:
2026-10-04
DOI:
10.3390/ijms27198852
Publication status:
Journal article

This Java workflow filters time-resolved m/z–intensity tracks using intensity, correlation, MinMax and early-change descriptors, retaining 29 tracks across three naringenin–ABTS reaction replicates. Its transparent trajectory prioritization complements existing LC–MS processing, but it omits chromatographic alignment, MS/MS, isotope and adduct relationships, so tracks need not represent distinct compounds. Thresholds were tuned to one reaction system, and chemical false-positive/negative rates were not established.

Reading scope: Indexed publisher text: selected Methods, Results and Limitations; supplements not read
