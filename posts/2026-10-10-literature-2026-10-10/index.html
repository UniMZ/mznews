Literature digest · October 10, 2026

Latest2026-10-10 · Beijing
English中文

Four recent papers examine proteomic representation learning, isotope-tracing feature pairing, DIA quantification benchmarks and isotope-guided metabolite discovery.

The most useful directions are realistic generalization tests and benchmarks with known quantitative changes. Coverage is partial: some ACS, MCP and Wiley pages were inaccessible; reading scope is stated for each paper.

ProFormer: generalizable classification of single-cell and plasma proteomes using deep learning

Karl K. Krull; Arlene Kühn; Julia Höhn; Syed Azmal Ali; Martin H. Otterbein; Titus J. Brinker; Jeroen Krijgsveld

Nature Communications · 2026

First online:
2026-10-05
DOI:
10.1038/s41467-026-78143-2
Publication status:
Peer-reviewed journal article.

ProFormer combines learnable peptide-ion aggregation with a transformer to classify samples from tabular MS1 features, including ion mobility. Its evaluation separates technical batch shifts from later biological and dose-response tests, making the paper useful for designing generalization benchmarks. The first test reused six cultures; DIA-NN/MaxQuant still supply MS2-informed features, and the plasma evaluation uses a random within-cohort split, not prospective clinical validation.

Reading scope: Publisher-indexed abstract and selected Results/Methods; supplements and code not audited. No independent reproduction.

IsoPairFinder: A Computational Workflow for Prioritizing LC–MS Feature Pairs Representing Candidate Microbial Pathway Intermediates from Stable Isotope Tracing Metabolomics

Zhiwei Zhou; Yuanyuan Liu; Mingxun Wang; Dylan Dodd

Journal of the American Society for Mass Spectrometry · 2026

First online:
2026-10-08
DOI:
10.1021/jasms.6c00253
Publication status:
Journal version of a 2025 preprint (10.1101/2025.08.18.670916). October 8 follows publisher metadata and journal homepage; an indexed contents page gives October 7.

IsoPairFinder combines differential analysis, redundant-feature consolidation and unlabeled/13C feature pairing to prioritize candidate microbial pathway intermediates. Its journal abstract demonstrates the workflow using Clostridium sporogenes uric-acid catabolism and reports R-package/GNPS2 implementations; prioritized pairs still require downstream structural validation.

Reading scope: Journal abstract and publisher-deposited metadata; journal full text and supplements unavailable.

Benchmarking ZenoSWATH-DIA and Rapid-Gradient Workflows for Plasma Proteomics

Haneen Alharbi; Amy Campbell; Richard D. Unwin

Journal of Proteome Research · 2026

First online:
2026-10-05
DOI:
10.1021/acs.jproteome.6c00487
Publication status:
Peer-reviewed article, online ahead of issue assignment.

This independent mixed-species benchmark varies E. coli abundance against constant human plasma to compare DIA-NN, Spectronaut and acquisition workflows by differential-abundance detection, rather than CV alone. The authors report that library-based DIA-NN with a 21-minute ZenoTOF 7600 gradient performed best in the tested setting. The transferable lesson is to benchmark true-change recovery; the result is specific to these samples and configurations, not a universal software ranking.

Reading scope: Official abstract, bibliographic metadata and supporting-information inventory; full text and numerical supplement not read.

LC−MS-Based Chlorine-Signature Query Engine Strategy for Automated Discovery of Phosphate-Containing Metabolites

Xiaolin Chen; Yujie Li; Lanjia Ao; Xiqing Bian; Haoyuan Zeng; Na Li; Jian-Lin Wu

Analytical Chemistry · 2026

First online:
2026-10-07
DOI:
10.1021/acs.analchem.6c01854
Publication status:
Peer-reviewed journal article.

Cl-SQE couples phosphate-directed chlorine tagging with automated mining of diagnostic isotope patterns, improving candidate discovery in LC–MS datasets. In cholestatic mouse liver, the abstract reports 318 potential derivatives and 189 metabolites identified or tentatively assigned; this hybrid chemistry/software method does not establish 189 confirmed identities.

Reading scope: Publisher-deposited abstract and metadata; full text, supplements and computational benchmarking not verified.
