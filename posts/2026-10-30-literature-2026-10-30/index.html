Literature Brief (October 30, 2026)

Latest2026-10-30 · Beijing
English中文

Peptide identification and immunogenicity prediction require distinct layers of evidence. Today’s paper connects mass-spectrometry searching, transcriptomics and candidate prioritization, with an emphasis on reuse and validation limits.

Accessible journal listings and indexes were checked across proteomics, metabolomics and small molecules, ion mobility, imaging and quality control. Access restrictions affected ACS, MCP, some Nature listings and preprints. Candidates lacking verified complete authorship, first-online dates or original scientific evidence were not included; coverage is not exhaustive.

MSIPep: A pipeline for immunogenic peptide identification and prioritization integrating immunopeptidomics and transcriptomics data

Peng Liu; Siwen Zhang; Manman Lu; Minrui Xu; Zhenhao Liu; Lanming Chen; Ping Xu; Xiaoxiu Tan; Lu Xie

BMC Genomics · 2026

First online:
2026-10-21
DOI:
10.1186/s12864-026-13481-5
Publication status:
Peer-reviewed accepted manuscript; not final Version of Record

MSIPep combines immunopeptidomics and transcriptomics with database search, de novo sequencing, multilevel FDR control and ProTCR-based prioritization of tumor peptides. Leukemia, ovarian-cancer and triple-negative breast-cancer applications support reuse, but predicted immunogenicity is not a measured immune response. The official README also notes that some external DIA tools are not wired into the current script, so deployments need to reconcile the reported workflow with its implementation.
