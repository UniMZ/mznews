MS2-KNN: A Computational Framework Resolving Chimeric Small-Molecule MS/MS Spectra by Latent-Space Neighborhood Decomposition and Scaffold Confidence Scoring

Yichen Wang; Luan Yin; Mengyue Hu; Minsong Guo; Junjie Qiu; Zichuan Wang; Huanjie Li; Yue Luo; Wenpei Chen; Xiao Zheng; Jie Liu; Yong Chen; Xuesong Liu; Su Zeng; Tengfei Xu

Analytical Chemistry · 2026

First online:
2026-11-24
DOI:
10.1021/acs.analchem.6c04590
Publication status:
Version of record

MS2-KNN groups library neighbors in an embedded spectral space using precursor m/z and structural scaffolds to resolve multiple contributors to a chimeric spectrum. Among the tested approaches it fully resolved the largest share of 88 measured mixtures from 43 standards; in synthetic tests the improvement depended on the true components being retrievable, making library coverage and weak signals important limitations.

Convergent Fragmentation: A Shared Ion Vocabulary Limits Structure−Spectrum Fidelity

Winnie Uritboonthai; Linh Hoang; Gary Siuzdak

Analytical Chemistry · 2026

First online:
2026-11-12
DOI:
10.1021/acs.analchem.6c05402
Publication status:
Version of record; earlier ChemRxiv preprint

Across 4,000 METLIN compound pairs, structural and spectral similarity had an R² of 0.28; all 56 recurrent fragment formulas were also observed in MoNA. Removing recurrent cations raised Spearman ρ from 0.410 to 0.468 in these data, but only aromatic and allylic cations carried less structural information than equally prevalent ordinary fragments, arguing against indiscriminate peak removal.

System-Independent Retention Index Prediction via Multimodal Molecular Representation Learning

Xujie Wang; Na An; Quanfei Zhu; Hongchao Ji; Yuqi Feng

Analytical Chemistry · 2026

First online:
2026-11-24
DOI:
10.1021/acs.analchem.6c05729
Publication status:
Version of record

MultiModal-RI combines a molecular graph model with a molecular language encoder to predict liquid chromatography retention indices from structure. Trained on 3,951 unique compounds, it reached MAE 60.81 and R² 0.94 on a separate reversed-phase test set; the prediction can narrow candidate lists, but this experiment does not establish transfer to other chromatographic modes or replace confirmatory evidence.

Exploring Functional Patterns in Mass Spectrometry Imaging Data with Spatial Pathway and Chemical Class Analysis (SPCCA)

Kuutti J. Vaananen; Timothy M. D. Ebbels

Journal of the American Society for Mass Spectrometry · 2026

First online:
2026-11-24
DOI:
10.1021/jasms.6c00401
Publication status:
Version of record

SPCCA turns individual ion images into spatial summaries of Reactome pathways or ChEBI chemical classes. In two tumor imaging datasets, pathway maps used sparse annotations at about 10% coverage, while class analysis used over 90% of filtered features and agreed more closely with histology; that agreement does not confirm individual metabolite identities or pathway activity.

Evaluation depth reshapes method rankings in proteomic co-abundance network inference

Shahram Aliyari; Nazanin Bagherlou; Zahra Salehi

bioRxiv · 2026

First online:
2026-11-18
DOI:
10.64898/2026.11.11.765733
Publication status:
Preprint v1

This benchmark compares five ways of inferring protein co-abundance networks and finds that in all six datasets supporting equal-depth comparisons, the leading method changed between the top 50 and the top 2,000 edges. That favors reporting performance across depths, but curated links are incomplete references for biological interactions; independent tests were mixed and each network was inferred just once.

MetaXtract: Extracting Metadata from RAW Files for FAIR Data Practices and Workflow Optimization

Ahmad Lutfi; Zhuo A. Chen; Lutz Fischer; Juri Rappsilber

Journal of Proteome Research · 2026

First online:
2026-11-18
DOI:
10.1021/acs.jproteome.6c00507
Publication status:
Version of record; earlier bioRxiv preprint

MetaXtract exports acquisition settings and scan metadata from Thermo RAW files, as well as spectral peak arrays linked to scans, for quality control without a database search. Across supported acquisition modes and files up to about 14 GB, the authors report 3.7–546.7 seconds and 0.51–5.90 GB peak memory for complete extraction; the benchmark does not establish compatibility with other vendors.
