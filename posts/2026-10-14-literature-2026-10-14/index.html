Literature digest · October 14, 2026

Latest2026-10-14 · Beijing
English中文

Today’s papers connect small-molecule spectral prediction, mobility-resolved peptide identification and transferable single-cell statistics.

The key questions are generalization beyond training chemistry and reliable precursor–fragment pairing. Some publisher full texts were inaccessible; coverage is incomplete.

SPHERE-MS: Hybrid Fragment–Vocabulary Modeling for Accurate and Efficient Tandem Mass Spectra Prediction

Yitong Guan; Doudou Lou; Dong Cheng; Mengyuan Liu; Yuan Zhu; Shuqiang Zhao; Xiangying Zhang

Analytical Chemistry · 2026

First online:
2026-10-13
DOI:
10.1021/acs.analchem.6c01723
Publication status:
Peer-reviewed ASAP article; follows preprint 10.26434/chemrxiv.10002044/v1

SPHERE-MS combines graph-neural-network bond breaking with a fragment/secondary-loss vocabulary to predict small-molecule MS/MS spectra without exhaustive bond-combination enumeration. The journal abstract reports better spectral prediction and retrieval than single-strategy baselines across NIST-20, MSnLib, CASMI2022 and MassSpecGym, but numerical gains and split/leakage controls could not be checked without the full paper. Official code supports protonated/deprotonated inputs with collision-energy and instrument settings; retraining requires separately licensed NIST-20 data.

Reading scope: Read publisher-deposited journal abstract and full bibliographic metadata in Crossref; checked official ACS ASAP listing, ChemRxiv version metadata and official repository README. Journal main text, supplementary benchmarks and preprint PDF were not obtained; no reproduction.

xTracer: Integrating Chromatogram and Mobilogram Correlations for Untargeted Peptide Identification in SLIM-Based PAMAF Data

Jian Song; Liulin Deng; Lauren Royer; Bennett Kalafut; Daniel DeBord; Jesse G. Meyer

Analytical Chemistry · 2026

First online:
2026-10-09
DOI:
10.1021/acs.analchem.6c01034
Publication status:
Peer-reviewed ASAP article; follows preprint 10.64898/2026.02.11.705440

xTracer pairs precursors and fragments using both chromatographic and ion-mobility correlations, converting quadrupole-free SLIM-PAMAF acquisitions into pseudo-spectra for conventional DDA database searching. The journal abstract reports improved peptide identification over single-domain correlation variants and diaTracer across the tested sample loads and throughputs; this does not establish superiority for conventional DDA/DIA acquisitions. Current software requires Windows and a separately obtained MOBILion MBI SDK, and the journal’s full benchmark details were unavailable for verification.

Reading scope: Read publisher-deposited journal abstract/full metadata, indexed primary preprint method/results/conclusion text, and current official repository README. Formal journal main text and supplements were not obtained; no software or experimental reproduction.

Quantifying distribution shifts in single-cell data with scXMatch

Anna Möller; Miriam Schnitzerlein; Eric Greto; Vasily Zaburdaev; Stefan Uderhardt; David B Blumenthal

Bioinformatics · 2026

First online:
2026-10-03
DOI:
10.1093/bioinformatics/btag706
Publication status:
Peer-reviewed journal article

scXMatch uses sparse nearest-neighbor graph matching to test whether two experimentally defined cell populations have different multivariate distributions, including shifts in spread. The authors evaluated RNA, chromatin-accessibility and cell-morphology data, reporting about 25 minutes and under 5 GB RAM for roughly 70,000 cells; application to single-cell proteomics remains an untested transfer opportunity. Inferred clusters must not be reused as test labels, and P-value rankings are not reliable effect-size rankings.

Reading scope: Targeted reading of official full-text HTML: author/history, abstract, method overview, benchmark results and discussion. Supplementary appendices not independently checked; no reproduction.
