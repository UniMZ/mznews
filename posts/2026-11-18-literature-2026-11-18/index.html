Literature Brief (November 18, 2026)

Latest2026-11-18 · Beijing
English中文

Today's papers address spatial mixture separation and the reuse of quantitative post-translational modification evidence. Reconstruction improvements and curation accuracy measure different tasks; neither establishes molecular-identification accuracy. Reading scopes are stated for each paper, and no results were independently reproduced.

Unmixing of imaging mass spectrometry measurements using microscopy-informed constraints

R. A. R. Moens; N. H. Patterson; L. G. Migas; A. B. Esselman-Lawrence; F. A. Moser; J. M. Spraggins; R. Van de Plas

Advances in Data Analysis and Classification · 2026

First online:
2026-11-09
DOI:
10.1007/s11634-026-00710-8
Publication status:
Journal article; a preprint appeared in 2025 (10.1101/2025.10.29.685281).

Microscopy-defined regions constrain low-rank nonnegative and sparse decomposition of mixed imaging mass spectrometry pixels, evaluated on synthetic data and kidney tissue. In the kidney example, reconstruction error fell from 80.09% to 22.71% over all pixels and from 33.18% to 29.84% over mixed pixels; these are reconstruction metrics, not molecular-identification accuracies. Spatial quantification may benefit, but the approach relies on within-region uniformity and registration assumptions, required about 60 hours on 56 cores in the reported computation, and its core algorithm already appeared in the 2025 preprint.

Reading scope: Publisher HTML methods and results, with comparison to the preprint; no reproduction.

qPTM 3.0: the AI-enhanced knowledgebase for protein post-translational modification dynamics

Jiamin Hu; Jun Wu; Dong Zhang; Yijin Wu; Yongqiang Zheng; Junteng Li; Minjin Zhan; Zeyao Wang; Nan Wu; Huanhuan He; Tianshun Gao; Qi Zhou; Ze-Xian Liu

Nucleic Acids Research · 2026

First online:
2026-11-17
DOI:
10.1093/nar/gkag1195
Publication status:
Journal article

qPTM 3.0 combines AI-assisted curation with manual review to organize about 15.65 million quantitative events and 995,000 modification sites across four species and 12 modification types. On an internal holdout of 37 papers, screening recall was 97.2%, while mean metadata and table-parsing accuracies were 94.1% and 94.2%; these are component-level, not end-to-end or external validation results. The resource supports reuse of modification dynamics, but cross-study comparisons still require normalization, and raw-spectrum reanalysis is not new to this version.

Reading scope: Publisher-indexed abstract and selected methods and evaluation details; no reproduction.
