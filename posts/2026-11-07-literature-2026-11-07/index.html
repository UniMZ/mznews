Literature Brief (November 7, 2026)

Latest2026-11-07 · Beijing
English中文

This brief covers FeatureMAP, PepPr and the GlycoPOST update, in that order.

Accessible primary-source abstracts, publication metadata and selected publisher-indexed sections were reviewed. Coverage is partial because some journal and preprint pages were inaccessible. Each entry states its actual reading scope; no software or results were reproduced.

FeatureMAP enables network-guided interpretation of untargeted metabolomics

Yuting Wang; Feifan Zhang; Guoping Peng; Lifeng Qiu; Li Zeng; Yijiang Liu; Jiramet Kinchagawat; Xicheng Yang; Chang Liu; Caroline Johnson; Qi Su; Chuchu Wang; Xiaotao Shen

bioRxiv · 2026

First online:
2026-11-05
DOI:
10.64898/2026.11.04.764165
Publication status:
Preprint, not peer-reviewed

FeatureMAP combines multi-evidence annotation, network-guided candidate refinement, metabolic-module discovery and structured LLM interpretation around LC–MS features. The abstract reports improved MS1 candidate prioritization and reduced redundancy across ten public datasets, with 92% recovery of one simulated perturbation in semi-synthetic benchmarking. Its companion PhenoMAP covers 12,929 dysregulated modules from 501 human studies spanning 326 phenotypes. It is relevant to interpreting incompletely annotated features. The 92% figure is not compound-identification accuracy; network-prior bias, dataset independence and LLM interpretation reliability require full-text assessment.

Reading scope: Publisher-deposited abstract, complete author metadata and posting date via Crossref, cross-checked against the official bioRxiv directory. Full text, supplements, software and reproduction were not assessed.

PepPr: De Novo peptide sequencing with peptide priors

Samuel Khan; Daniela Klaproth-Andrade; Yanik Bruns; Mathias Wilhelm; Julien Gagneur

bioRxiv · 2026

First online:
2026-10-28
DOI:
10.64898/2026.10.27.762126
Publication status:
Preprint, not peer-reviewed

PepPr combines de novo sequencing with a reference-proteome peptide-language-model prior. The abstract reports improved reference and variant identification and recovery of some antibody regions. Its attraction is combining reference knowledge with discovery; prior bias, error control, leakage and cross-species generalization still require scrutiny. Abstract-level evidence does not support a quantitative performance claim.

Reading scope: Indexed primary-source abstract and complete author list only. Full-text access was unavailable; supplementary materials and software were not assessed, and no reproduction was performed.

GlycoPOST: expanding glycomics data sharing to glycoproteomics through the updated MIRAGE guidelines

Yushi Takahashi; Zekun Wang; Ryoichi Noguchi; Akira Seno; Kiyoko F. Aoki-Kinoshita; Shujiro Okuda

Nucleic Acids Research · 2026

First online:
2026-11-05
DOI:
10.1093/nar/gkag1126
Publication status:
Peer-reviewed journal article

GlycoPOST extends raw MS data sharing from glycomics to glycoproteomics through revised MIRAGE reporting requirements, project types and metadata-preset filtering, including projects linking both experimental approaches. As of 1 September 2026, it held 602 projects and 7.6 TiB of data, with 445 projects and 5.2 TiB public. Its development value lies in structured experimental metadata and data reuse. The repository does not interpret or curate glycan or glycopeptide identifications; machine-readable standards for identification results and confidence remain unfinished. Each new project type had only one public example at that cutoff, so these counts do not establish broad adoption or improved identification accuracy.

Reading scope: Publisher-indexed abstract, project statistics, update features, limitations and author contributions, plus the publisher's advance-article listing. The complete article and supplementary materials were not reviewed; the platform was not tested and results were not reproduced.
