Multi-Enzyme Data-Independent Acquisition Mass Spectrometry for Proteoform-Aware Single-shot Proteomics

Moritz Heusel; Lotta Happonen; Christofer Karlsson; Emma Åhrman; Johan Malmström

bioRxiv · 2026

First online:
2026-11-09
DOI:
10.64898/2026.11.06.764873
Publication status:
Preprint, v1

mE-DIA multiplexes complementary enzyme digests into one DIA injection, integrating library-free analysis with proteoform inference and an interactive peptide mapper. On the same raw data and within a shared search space, the authors report 30% more identified protein groups and 47% more groups quantified at CV ≤20% than their DDA-library-based multi-enzyme DIA comparator. Gains are comparison-specific; inferred backbone variants are not experimentally established complete proteoforms.

Three-dimensional mass spectral libraries for improved metabolites identification using collision energy profiles of protonated and radical cation precursors

Patrick Mueller; Gérard Hopfgartner

Analytical and Bioanalytical Chemistry · 2026

First online:
2026-11-11
DOI:
10.1007/s00216-026-06913-4
Publication status:
Journal article

Collision-energy profiles add a third dimension to spectral libraries, exploiting complementary fragmentation of radical cations and protonated precursors. Across 40 compounds, 79% of the ten most abundant formula losses differed between precursor types; SWATH profile matching was demonstrated with nine substances spiked into plasma. Validation remains small-scale and search software needs adaptation.

Reading scope: Indexed publisher abstract and selected text; not full methods or independently reproduced.
