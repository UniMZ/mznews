Literature Brief (November 4, 2026)

Latest2026-11-04 · Beijing
English中文

Spectral structure elucidation and quality control both need inspectable evidence. This brief examines the limits of LLMs assisted by specialist tools, diagnostic reporters in molecular networks, and reproducible DIA quality checks.

Accessible journal listings, publication metadata and indexes covered proteomics, small molecules and lipids, ion mobility, imaging and signal processing. Some publisher and preprint articles were inaccessible; the interpretations below retain explicit abstract-level and validation limits.

LLMs Depend on Specialist Tools for Out-of-Distribution Generalization in MS/MS Structural Elucidation

Gabriel Asher; Cailum M. K. Stienstra; Tomo Oga; Devesh Shah; Antonio H. de O. Fonseca; Niall O’Connor; Michael Widrich

arXiv · 2026

First online:
2026-11-02
DOI:
10.48550/arXiv.2611.03308
Publication status:
Preprint v1

A 100-compound MassSpecGym benchmark matches acquisition conditions while separating compounds by textual footprint. Tool ablations raise textually out-of-distribution Top-1 accuracy from 2% without tools to 26% with MS-GPT and 38% with proprietary LSM-Gen, pointing to candidate generation as a bottleneck. Textual footprint is only a proxy for training exposure, not chemical novelty; molecular formulas are supplied, one LLM is tested, and the agent costs roughly twenty times the generator alone, making candidate coverage and deployment cost essential evaluation targets.

Reading scope: Main-text methods, results and limitations read; author page checked. Not reproduced; appendices not reviewed in full.

Identification of diagnostic reporters in MS/MS Molecular Networks

Nicola Dalla Valle; Pietro Franceschi; Mar Garcia-Aloy; Peter Robatscher; Sabine Oettl; Michael Oberhuber

Analytical Chemistry · 2026

First online:
2026-11-02
DOI:
10.1021/acs.analchem.6c03306
Publication status:
Journal article

Network modularity and quantile generalised additive models identify fragment ions and neutral losses whose network cohesion exceeds that expected from their prevalence. The abstract reports prioritisation of tricycloalternarene-specific reporters in an Alternaria metabolome, offering interpretable evidence for annotation propagation. Only the abstract was accessible, so cross-dataset robustness and propagation-error control remain unassessed.

Reading scope: Publisher-deposited Crossref abstract and metadata only; full text and supplements unavailable, not reproduced.

QC-espresso: Brewing quality in proteomics

Guilherme Pauperio Lanfredi; Jackson Gabriel Miyamoto

bioRxiv · 2026

First online:
2026-11-02
DOI:
10.64898/2026.10.29.762921
Publication status:
Preprint, not peer-reviewed; first-posted date verified from bioRxiv-deposited metadata

QC-espresso converts DIA-NN report.parquet output and FASTA data into standardized HTML quality-control reports, run statistics and exportable tables using R and Quarto. Three experimental datasets illustrate associations involving contamination, chromatographic changes and systematic mass-error bias; reproducible inspection is valuable, but these examples do not establish independently validated fault-detection accuracy or causality from co-occurring metrics.

Reading scope: bioRxiv-deposited Crossref abstract, complete authors and first-posted metadata; original page unavailable, full text and supplements not read, no reproduction.
