Literature Brief (November 1, 2026)

Latest2026-11-01 · Beijing
English中文

Today’s focus is metabolite-candidate prioritization in mass spectrometry imaging: combining spatial image similarity with chemical relationships to guide follow-up while distinguishing ranked candidates from definitive structural identification.

Accessible journal listings, publication metadata and indexes were checked across proteomics, small molecules and lipids, ion mobility, imaging, signal processing and statistics. Access was incomplete for ACS, MCP, some Nature pages and preprints, and indexed coverage was not exhaustive. Candidates with unresolved complete authors, first-public dates or substantive version changes were not included.

Integrated Prior Knowledge by Bayesian Inference for Spatial Metabolomics Annotation in Mass Spectrometry Imaging

Baolin Xiong; Jishuai Wang; Yuanqing Zhang; Lei Wang; Peipei Zhou; Fangfang Wang; Xin Qi; Yu Guo; Wenchang Xu; Yibo Chen; Zhi Sun; Wenbo Cheng

Analytical Chemistry · 2026

First online:
2026-10-30
DOI:
10.1021/acs.analchem.5c08132
Publication status:
Peer-reviewed journal article, online first

BIMSM combines exact-mass matching probabilities with adduct and reaction relationships calibrated by ion-image similarity to prioritize MSI metabolite candidates through Bayesian inference. Among evaluable mouse-brain reference candidates, Top-1/3/5 coverage rose from 6.5%/14.4%/20.0% to 17.9%/27.4%/32.9%; this supports spatial–chemical prior integration, not definitive identification or whole-metabolome accuracy, and orthogonal validation remains necessary.

Reading scope: Publisher-deposited Crossref abstract, author list and first-online metadata only; the publisher article was inaccessible, and full text, supplements and reproducibility were not assessed.
