Defining state-selective lipid binding to brain GPCRs using REVEAL

Sophie A. S. Lawrence; Carla Kirschbaum; Corinne A. Lutomski; Haigang Song; Joshua D. Hinkle; Frances I. Butroid; Rafael Melani; John. E. P. Syka; Christopher Mullen; Tarick J. El-Baba; Carol. V. Robinson

Nature Chemical Biology · 2026

First online:
2026-11-27
DOI:
10.1038/s41589-026-02402-y
Publication status:
Journal article

REVEAL reconstructs release profiles for individual lipids across precursor isolation windows, then compares them with the receptor MS1 envelope using a permutation threshold and a signal-to-noise filter. In a brain lipid extract with over 1,000 components, the authors tested two neuronal receptors and classified 48 lipid annotations as specific mGlyR binders. Acquisition is automated, but some profile extraction and structural annotation remain manual, and these experiments do not establish binding in vivo.

A reference-standard-free semi-quantitative workflow for pesticide residue analysis by LC-QTOF/MS using machine learning-based prediction of ionization efficiency: application to banana screening

Kaixuan Tong; Tongxin Zhi; Meiling Lu; Xingqiang Wu; Yujie Xie; Mengjie Shi; Hui Chen

Analytical Methods · 2026

First online:
2026-11-24
DOI:
10.1039/d6ay01823h
Publication status:
Advance online article

The workflow predicts relative ionization efficiency from molecular descriptors and mobile-phase properties, then converts LC-QTOF/MS signals to approximate concentrations. Gradient boosting reached a test-set R² of 0.756; estimates for 11 detected pesticides in an external banana proficiency sample had a median error of 2.74-fold. This supports order-of-magnitude screening, not replacement of targeted quantification or transfer across matrices.

Accelerated Characterization of Active Natural Extracts with Single-Step microfractionation Coupled to Mass Spectrometry and Bioassays

Joshua David Smith; Erik Bouchal; Zdeněk Knejzlík; Martin Dračínský; Tito Damiani; Roman Bushuiev; Eva Tikalová; Eva Tloušťová; Marcela Pávová; Jan Hodek; Artur Jasanský; Matouš Soldát; Erika Kužmová; Alžběta Kadlecová; Vendula Tvrdoňová Stillerová; Jan Weber; Pavel Šácha; Tomáš Pluskal; Téo Hebra

Analytical Chemistry · 2026

First online:
2026-11-27
DOI:
10.1021/acs.analchem.6c04527
Publication status:
Journal article

This microfractionation workflow uses an open-source graphical interface to combine single-quadrupole and high-resolution MS data with fraction-level bioactivity, narrowing the candidates for structural follow-up. The journal article expands the earlier preprint’s two plant species to seven and reports 17 structures from proton NMR dereplication. Co-occurrence in a fraction helps prioritize candidates but does not identify the compound responsible for activity on its own.

This multisite benchmark evaluated 24 tissue-preparation and staining combinations using signal, variability, and marker failure. A subset of conditions was validated on optical platforms and imaging mass cytometry. The results inform image-based QC and experimental design, but the study primarily compares sample preparation rather than introducing a segmentation method or establishing transfer to every tissue and antibody.
