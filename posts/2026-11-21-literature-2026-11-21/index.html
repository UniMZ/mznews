Latest2026-11-21 · Beijing

Three methods address different sources of variation in mass spectrometry: response to concentration, shifts along the mass axis, and shifts along the retention-time axis.

Aletheia: an open-source pipeline for QC dilution-based feature filtering in untargeted LC–MS metabolomics and lipidomics

Ángela Peralbo-Molina; Cristina María López; Ana Salinas-Gavilán; Eduardo Chicano-Gálvez

Metabolomics · 2026

First online:
2026-11-20
DOI:
10.1007/s11306-026-02560-z
Publication status:
Journal article

Aletheia uses pooled-QC dilution series to filter LC–MS features and records the reason for each retention, rejection, or flag. In synthetic plasma, it rejected features that had already passed a repeatability filter, showing that the two checks address different properties. A positive slope from two points does not establish linearity, and some negative responses were retained with a flag.

PGAlign: Peak Group Alignment of Mass Spectrometry Data

Anji Deng; Yuyang Zhang; Qihuang Zhang

Journal of Proteome Research · 2026

First online:
2026-11-09
DOI:
10.1021/acs.jproteome.6c00499
Publication status:
Journal article

PGAlign matches local peak groups rather than isolated peaks to map MALDI imaging datasets onto a shared m/z grid for joint analysis across tissue sections. Companion scripts demonstrate imposed m/z shifts in pancreas data and pixel classification within tissue pairs. The latter does not hold out specimens and therefore does not establish cross-specimen generalization.

DEPI-MS: Density-Equalized Pseudo-Image Learning for Drift-Tolerant LC–MS Metabolomics Classification

Xiaohui Lin; Shuang Gao; Jie Yang; Xunhua Huang; Xiumei Gu; Xiangnan Xu; Kian-Kai Cheng; Aihua Ye; Zhaodong Fei; Daniel Raftery; Jiyang Dong

Analytical Chemistry · 2026

First online:
2026-11-12
DOI:
10.1021/acs.analchem.6c03243
Publication status:
Journal article

DEPI-MS represents LC–MS signals as pseudo-images with uniform retention-time bins and m/z resolution allocated by signal density in QC samples. Training applies differentiable retention-time warping. The indexed abstract describes an external nasopharyngeal carcinoma serum validation set and a drift stress test, but does not establish transfer to other instruments or acquisition conditions.
