Today’s focus is an LC–HRMS workflow connecting signal extraction, feature construction and putative metabolite annotation.

Further reading should examine cross-instrument generalization, parameter dependence and benchmark conditions. Coverage is incomplete because some journal and full-text sources were inaccessible.

Emzing: A Deep Learning-Enabled Software Suite for End-to-End Analysis of High-Resolution LC-MS Metabolomics Data

Aviral Singh; Kasturi Nandi; Prerna Dubey; Nived Krishnan Santhosh; Shayantan Banerjee; Prajval Nakrani; Avinash Vellore Sunder; Deepti Sahasrabuddhe; Pramod P. Wangikar

Analytical Chemistry · 2026

First online:
2026-10-12
DOI:
10.1021/acs.analchem.6c02696
Publication status:
Peer-reviewed ASAP journal article, preceded by MSOne/Emzing preprint 10.26434/chemrxiv-2025-g03kr/v4.

Emzing integrates deep-learning peak detection, adaptive noise estimation, retention-time alignment and graph grouping for LC–HRMS metabolomics, with MSI level-2 putative library annotations. The abstract reports benchmark improvements and over 110 reprocessed public datasets, but full-text comparison conditions were unavailable for assessment and putative matches are not confirmed identities.

Reading scope: Official listing, publisher abstract/metadata, software page and preprint relationship checked; full text unavailable and no independent reproduction.
