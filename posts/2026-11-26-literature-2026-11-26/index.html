For method development, assess candidate coverage, independent structural confirmation and quantitative agreement separately. The assessments here are based on the abstracts indicated for each paper, not full-text review or reproduction.

Deciphering Molecular Language for Spectral Interpretation with a Foundation Model Pretrained on Repository-Wide Metabolomics Data

Yifan Li; Yunhua Zhong; Pan Liu; Zikun Wang; Jiaqing Xie; Hongxin Xiang; Zihan Chen; Zhiyuan Yuan; Yuqiang Li; Jun Xia

bioRxiv · 2026

First online:
2026-11-24
DOI:
10.64898/2026.11.14.766667
Publication status:
Preprint v1

UltraMS learns representations from more than 160 million tandem spectra in positive and negative ion modes, then transfers annotations to suggest substructures for individual peaks and organize spectra in UltraAtlas. The authors report gains across several downstream tasks, but transferred peak assignments are structural hypotheses, not direct confirmation; the abstract does not establish how repository overlap was controlled.

Fragment anchoring for metabolite annotation beyond whole-spectrum similarity

Young Jae Park; Ji Hyun Moon; Ki Tae Suk; Do Yup Lee

bioRxiv · 2026

First online:
2026-11-19
DOI:
10.64898/2026.11.11.766011
Publication status:
Preprint v1

ANCHor searches for fragments enriched among related reference spectra and combines them with mass differences and transformation rules. It recovered 147 of 166 methionine-related spectra in one test, including 93 with modified cosine similarity below 0.70, and at least one anchor was available for 76.9% of 1,021 standards; these are coverage and recall results, not an identification error rate.

GlycanPRMQuant: Automated Identification and Quantification of Permethylated N-Glycans from LC-PRM-MS/MS Data

Vishal Sandilya; Sherifdeen Onigbinde; Sarah Sahioun; Abiodun Adewolu; Ronny Barrera; Yehia Mechref

Analytical Chemistry · 2026

First online:
2026-11-16
DOI:
10.1021/acs.analchem.6c04973
Publication status:
Version of record

GlycanPRMQuant uses diagnostic fragments, coelution evidence and charge-aware target–decoy competition to resolve competing assignments for permethylated N-glycans, leaving unresolved features ambiguous. Quantification agreed closely with manually curated Skyline integration (Pearson R 0.992–1.000), but the reported replicate results are for bovine fetuin and ribonuclease B glycans, not a broad test of structural assignment.

Reading scope: Publisher-deposited abstract

ProteoBridge Connects Proteomics Quantification Outputs to Reusable Analysis Workflows

Witold E. Wolski; Robbe Devreese; Robbin Bouwmeester; Henry Webel; Leonardo Schwarz; Caroline Jachmann; Sam van Puyenbroeck; Christian Panse; Hubert Rehrauer; Lennart Martens; Marie Locard-Paulet

bioRxiv · 2026

First online:
2026-11-24
DOI:
10.64898/2026.11.23.768141
Publication status:
Preprint v1

ProteoBridge maps the different output fields and parameter files of 14 bottom-up quantification tools into common feature levels, with machine-readable provenance and warnings. This can simplify downstream analysis across software and versions, but harmonizing tables does not make upstream abundances or statistics interchangeable.

Reading scope: Preprint abstract deposited with Crossref

QC Shotgun reports eight relative metrics in a test lasting under five minutes, intended to track high-resolution shotgun lipidomics instruments over time and make local setups easier to compare. The metrics flag performance changes but do not establish quantitative agreement for every lipid class or substitute for sample-specific quality controls.

Unipept in 2026: A Unified Web Platform for Comparative Metaproteomics Analysis

Tibo Vande Moortele; Simon Van de Vyver; Ben-Bjoern Binke; Tim Van Den Bossche; Lennart Martens; Pieter Verschaffelt; Peter Dawyndt; Bart Mesuere

bioRxiv · 2026

First online:
2026-11-24
DOI:
10.64898/2026.11.23.768510
Publication status:
Preprint v1

Unipept moves comparative metaproteomics projects into the browser, adds pathway views and uses the NORI inference engine to support confidence estimates. The authors illustrate the workflow with six datasets from a defined microbial community; those examples do not establish that the confidence estimates are calibrated in more complex communities.

LipidRT: leveraging retention time coherence for robust lipid annotation in reversed-phase LC-MS-based lipidomics

Celia Regal-Marqués; María Isabel Alcoriza-Balaguer; Hana Cermakova; Marta Roca; Óscar Juan-Vidal; Julián Carretero; Ana Gradillas; Coral Barbas; Juan Carlos García-Cañaveras; Agustín Lahoz

First online:
2026-11-18
DOI:
10.64898/2026.11.11.765883
Publication status:
Preprint v1

LipidRT uses expected relative elution patterns to screen lipid annotations and excludes retention-inconsistent assignments from its dataset-specific model fit. It adds useful evidence for reversed-phase lipidomics, but the abstract gives no quantitative error rate or evidence of transfer beyond that chromatographic setting.
