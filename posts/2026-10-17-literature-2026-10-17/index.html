Today's papers examine spectral evidence for de novo candidate reranking and stricter validation of retention-time prediction.

The key direction is connecting model outputs to verifiable evidence and matching evaluation splits to the intended deployment setting.

This selection draws on accessible journal listings and publisher-indexed records; access restrictions prevented complete coverage of some ACS, MCP and Nature pages.

ScopeNovo: a De Novo Peptide Sequencing Method Using Spectrum-Spectrum Similarity to Refine the Prediction Accuracy

Zhi Song; Zejian Ding; Lusheng Wang

Bioinformatics · 2026

First online:
2026-10-15
DOI:
10.1093/bioinformatics/btag755
Publication status:
Accepted manuscript

ScopeNovo reranks π-PrimeNovo candidates using spectral similarity, prioritizing library spectra and falling back to Prosit predictions. In the well-annotated setting, the authors report peptide accuracies of 0.711/0.737 on two nine-species benchmarks, versus 0.687/0.682 for RankNovo. Separating candidate generation from spectral verification is useful, but library availability, library–test separation and error calibration need scrutiny; accuracy gains do not establish FDR control.

Reading scope: Publisher abstract and repository README; manuscript, supplementary analyses and code execution not checked

Hybrid Graph Neural Network Ensemble with Stacked Meta-Learning for Laboratory-Conditioned Chromatographic Retention-Time Prediction

Shengnan Wang; Qi Zhang; Xucheng Liu; Zixuan Wang; Jiangke Cheng; Haolong Hong

The Journal of Physical Chemistry B · 2026

First online:
2026-10-15
DOI:
10.1021/acs.jpcb.6c03944
Publication status:
Peer-reviewed article, online ahead of issue

This retention-time model combines GAT, GCN and fingerprint networks with ExtraTrees and learned laboratory embeddings. Across 3,776 observations from 23 laboratories, the authors report MAE values of 0.360 minutes under molecular-identity grouping and 0.670 minutes under scaffold-aware validation. Identity-safe splits and explicit ensemble comparisons are particularly useful, but the demonstrated scope is new chemical groups within represented laboratory labels, not generalization to unseen laboratories.

Reading scope: Indexed publisher abstract, author metadata and supplementary-material description; full text and supplementary analyses not checked
