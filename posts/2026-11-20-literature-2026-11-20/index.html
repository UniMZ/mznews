Literature Brief (November 20, 2026)

Latest2026-11-20 · Beijing
English中文

A more accurate fingerprint need not retrieve the correct structure more often, and a valid isotope pair is not proof of glycan composition. This brief connects both distinctions with practical guidance on choosing public metabolomics data for benchmarks.

Coverage also included proteomics, ion mobility and imaging; some publisher material and conference abstracts were not available to verify.

On objective mismatch in molecular retrieval from tandem mass spectrometry

Gaetan De Waele; Marek Wydmuch; Krzysztof Dembczyński; Wojciech Kotłowski; Willem Waegeman

arXiv · 2026

First online:
2026-02-18
DOI:
10.48550/arXiv.2602.16507
Publication status:
arXiv v2 (November 9, 2026; listed in the official NeurIPS 2026 poster program)

The study separates fingerprint prediction accuracy from molecular retrieval and adds Enveda-180 and Transformer experiments in the November revision. On mass-matched Enveda-180 candidates, Transformer top-1 hit rate was 43.14% with IoU loss versus 63.11% with a contrastive fingerprint-cosine loss; fingerprint Tanimoto was lower for the latter, and its retrieval and fingerprint metrics used separately tuned models. Select models against the downstream retrieval metric, but note that this benchmark assumes the correct structure is present among the candidates.

Reading scope: Methods and results, with v1/v2 comparison; not reproduced

MS-GDF: A Framework for Automated Identification of Stable Isotope-Derivatized Glycans through Deep Denoising and Two-Level Evidence Integration

Caixia Yuan; Xiaoyu Qian; Ying Jin; Jiajin Yi; Tian Lin; Liuxin Dong; Dandan Zhang; Xi Luo; Cai Tie; Xue Bai; Haiyan Lyu; Yingjie Chen; Junyang Liu; Feng Ye; Jiyang Dong; Caisheng Wu

Analytical Chemistry · 2026

First online:
2026-11-10
DOI:
10.1021/acs.analchem.6c05311
Publication status:
Published online

MS-GDF filters MS1 signals by charge and isotope cluster, then combines light/heavy pairing with MS/MS glycone mass matching to assign glycan compositions. In a human plasma N-glycan benchmark, MS1 feature counts fell by 77.5% and 73.7% in the light- and heavy-labeled data, and manual review confirmed 95.1% of Level 1 candidates as valid pairs. This informs how to remove redundant features from isotope-labeled data, but the pair-confirmation rate does not measure glycan composition accuracy.

MetaboLights in 2027: public metabolomics data at scale

Thomas Payne; Felix X Amaladoss; Jonathan E Hunter; Ibrahim Karaman; Callum Martin; Naveen R K Polliapram; Noemi Tejera; Mark Williams; Ozgur Yurekten; Juan Antonio Vizcaíno

Nucleic Acids Research · 2026

First online:
2026-11-19
DOI:
10.1093/nar/gkag1199
Publication status:
Published online

The MetaboLights update adds locally runnable metadata validation, mzTab-M to ISA-Tab conversion, and a way to use study-search results as input for compound queries, helping researchers find and prepare public benchmark data. The authors found 177 method or tool development papers among 274 manually confirmed reuse publications through December 2025. That count describes reuse, not algorithm performance, and passing metadata checks does not replace checking acquisition conditions and annotations before benchmarking.

Reading scope: Submission, search and reuse sections; not reproduced
