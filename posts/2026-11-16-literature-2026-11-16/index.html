Latest2026-11-16 · Beijing

Today's selections cover isotope-distribution modelling with modification handling and changes to a protein-quantification package. Evidence comes from publisher metadata, author software documentation and official release notes; quantitative benchmarks and independent replication were not assessed.

Pointless 2.0: A Fast Compositional Modelling Framework to Predict the Isotope Distribution for Average Biomolecules with a Convolution Operator for Modification Handling

Frédérique Vilenne; Piotr Prostko; Dirk Valkenborg

Analytical Chemistry · 2026

First online:
2026-11-14
DOI:
10.1021/acs.analchem.6c03282
Publication status:
Journal article

Pointless 2.0 uses OCR transformation and low-order regression to predict isotope distributions for average DNA, RNA and peptides, with IsoSwap convolution for sulfur and chemical or post-translational modifications. It can estimate monoisotopic mass from average mass. Quantitative benchmarks were not verified, and average-composition predictions do not replace exact calculations from known formulas.

Reading scope: Publisher metadata and author software documentation; full paper not read and results not reproduced.

Protein Quantification in Mass Spectrometry-Based Proteomics

Thang V. Pham; Alex A. Henneman

CRAN · 2026

First online:
2026-11-06
DOI:
10.32614/CRAN.package.iq
Publication status:
Software revision: iq 2.2.0 (November 6); CRAN currently lists 2.3.0 (November 14), whose changes were not verified.

iq 2.2.0 unifies text, Parquet and data-frame inputs, introduces streaming and parallel batch quantification, switches MaxLFQ routines to the maxlfq_bit solver and removes Rcpp/RcppEigen dependencies. The release notes do not establish independent accuracy or speed improvements.

Reading scope: Official package metadata and NEWS; software not run and results not reproduced. Alex A. Henneman is credited as a contributor.
