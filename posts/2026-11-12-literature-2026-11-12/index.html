Literature Brief (November 12, 2026)

Latest2026-11-12 · Beijing
English中文

This brief examines representation learning for small-molecule tandem mass spectra, focusing on retrieval across instruments and datasets.

Interpretation is based on the ACS-indexed abstract and bibliographic metadata only. Full text was unavailable; supplementary materials were not read, and the method was not tested or reproduced.

SpecRoFormer: Relative Position-Aware Representation Learning for Small-Molecule MS/MS Spectra

Guoqing Zhu; Shiting Lai; Xinyi Cao; Keyu Hu; Luojun Lin; Yuanlong Yu

Analytical Chemistry · 2026

First online:
2026-10-30
DOI:
10.1021/acs.analchem.6c03195
Publication status:
Peer-reviewed journal article

SpecRoFormer combines relative spectral modeling in a Transformer with fragmentation-aware augmentation to learn representations for cross-instrument small-molecule MS/MS retrieval. It is trained on GNPS and evaluated on MoNA, MassBank, MassSpecGym and simulated SimMS-9M spectra. The abstract reports stronger overall retrieval than MSBERT, DreaMS and SpecEmbedding, without numerical gains available in the evidence read. The approach suggests a useful direction for encoding relationships among spectral peaks rather than relying only on isolated peak information. Whether the evaluation splits adequately prevent leakage, how large the improvements are, and how well the method generalizes to real-world samples require full-text assessment. Abstract-level results do not establish universal superiority across instruments or chemical spaces.
